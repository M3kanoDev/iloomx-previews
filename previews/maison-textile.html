The Sunday room

Caring for linen

Washing

Machine wash at 40°C with a mild detergent. Skip fabric softener; linen softens by itself.

Drying

Line dry in the shade, or tumble dry low and take it out while slightly damp.

Ironing

Not needed. The relaxed crease is the point. For a crisp look, iron on medium while damp.

Storing

Keep in a cool, dry cupboard and avoid plastic so the fibres can breathe.
