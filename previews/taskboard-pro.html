FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I import from Trello or Jira?

Yes. Connect your account and import boards, cards, comments and attachments in a few minutes.

What happens when the trial ends?

You choose a plan or move to Starter. Your boards and data stay exactly as they are.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in the EU, with daily backups and SSO on Business.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes. Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off Pro and Business.
